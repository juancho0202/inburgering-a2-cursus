The A2 inburgering exam is not one exam but several separate exams. You book and pass each one on its own. Mijn Inburgering shows which ones you have to take.

## The parts

- **Reading (Lezen):** on a computer. You read texts and answer questions. 65 minutes.
- **Listening (Luisteren):** on a computer. You answer questions about short films, and you listen to texts. 45 minutes.
- **Writing (Schrijven):** with **pen and paper**. You get 4 tasks, such as writing a short letter or filling in a form. 40 minutes.
- **Speaking (Spreken):** on a computer. You watch short films and answer questions by speaking Dutch. 35 minutes.
- **KNM (Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij):** knowledge of Dutch society, on a computer. The questions are grouped in themes, such as living, and work and income. 45 minutes.

Some people integrating under the older 2013 law also take **ONA** (orientation on the Dutch labour market). Under the 2021 law you don't. Mijn Inburgering shows which law applies to you.

The details above are from DUO's pages on the [language exams](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/content-language-exams-a2-b1-b2.jsp) and the [knowledge exams](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/content-knowledge-exams.jsp); check there before your exam.

## Free official practice exams

DUO has free **official practice exams** for every A2 part and for KNM on its [practice page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/practicing.jsp): 3 for Writing, 3 for Speaking, 3 for Listening, 4 for Reading and 2 for KNM. They look like the real exam, so do them at least once before your exam.

- Use a computer if you can. On a phone or tablet, turn it sideways.
- The Speaking practice exams don't work in Safari: use another browser.
- Don't learn the answers by heart: the real exam has different questions.

## Practise here, for free

[Train for Inburgering](/) gives you lessons and exercises for Reading, Listening, Writing and KNM at A2 level, and timed practice exams with a score at the end. The explanations are in English, Dutch or Spanish; the exercises are in Dutch, like the real exam. Speaking is not in the app yet: use DUO's practice exams for that.

## Tips

- Practise with a timer, so the real exam does not feel rushed.
- For Writing, practise on paper: short letters to a neighbour, a school or a landlord, and filling in forms.
- For KNM, learn how things work in daily life: the doctor, the municipality (gemeente), renting a home, school and work.
- Book your exam early: it can take more than 6 weeks before you can take it. See [how to book your exam](/guides/book-inburgering-exam/).
