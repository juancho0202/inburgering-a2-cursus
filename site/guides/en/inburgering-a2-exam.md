The A2 inburgering exam is not one exam but several separate exams. You book and pass each one on its own. Mijn Inburgering shows which ones you have to do.

## The parts

- **Reading (Lezen):** on a computer. You read short texts and answer questions. About 65 minutes.
- **Listening (Luisteren):** on a computer. You watch short videos and listen to texts, then answer questions. About 45 minutes.
- **Writing (Schrijven):** short writing tasks, such as a short message or filling in a form. About 40 minutes.
- **Speaking (Spreken):** on a computer. You watch short videos and record your answers. About 35 minutes. Since 1 March 2025 there are no multiple-choice questions in this exam any more.
- **KNM (Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij):** knowledge of Dutch society, such as work, health, housing, education and the government. Multiple-choice questions on a computer.
- **ONA** (orientation on the Dutch labour market): only for some people. Mijn Inburgering tells you if you need it.

The times above are what DUO lists on its [language exams page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/content-language-exams-a2-b1-b2.jsp); check there before your exam.

## Free official practice exams

DUO has free **official practice exams** for every A2 part and for KNM on its [practice page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/practicing.jsp). They look like the real exam screens, so do them at least once before your exam. Don't learn the answers by heart: the real exam has different questions.

## Practise here, for free

[Train for Inburgering](/) gives you lessons and exercises for Reading, Listening, Writing and KNM at A2 level, and timed practice exams with a score at the end. The explanations are in English, Dutch or Spanish; the exercises are in Dutch, like the real exam. Speaking is not in the app yet: use DUO's practice exams for that.

## Tips

- Practise with a timer, so the real exam does not feel rushed.
- For Writing, practise short messages to a neighbour, a school or a landlord, and filling in forms.
- For KNM, learn how things work in daily life: the doctor, the municipality (gemeente), renting a home, school and work.
- Book your exam early: it can take more than 6 weeks before there is a place. See [how to book your exam](/guides/book-inburgering-exam/).
