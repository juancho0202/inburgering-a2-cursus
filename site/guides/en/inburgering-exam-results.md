After your exam, DUO sends your result by letter. You can also see it online in **Mijn Inburgering**.

## When you get your result

- **A2 language exams and KNM:** within **8 weeks**, by letter and in Mijn Inburgering.
- **Speaking A2 and Writing A2** can take longer, because people check your answers. When it is busy DUO extends this period; it has said up to 16 weeks. DUO's [results page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/results.jsp) has the current time.
- **ONA:** the result is in Mijn Inburgering.
- **State exam NT2 (B1 or B2):** this result is in **Mijn DUO**, not in Mijn Inburgering.

## How to see your result online

1. [Log in to Mijn Inburgering](/guides/mijn-inburgering-login/) with DigiD.
2. Open your exams. Your result is shown next to each exam you took.

## Waiting while your deadline comes closer

You have a fixed period to finish your integration. If that period is almost over while you are still waiting for a result of Speaking A2 or Writing A2, DUO has given extra time. Check DUO's [results page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/results.jsp) for what applies to you now.

## Did not pass?

You can book the exam again in Mijn Inburgering: see [how to book your exam](/guides/book-inburgering-exam/). A new attempt usually costs money again. Use the time to practise the parts you found hard.

## Passed everything?

When you have passed all the exams you need, DUO sends you your diploma. DUO's results pages explain when and how.
