You book the A2 language exams, the KNM exam and the ONA exam yourself, in **Mijn Inburgering**. You choose the exam, a date and a location.

## Before you book

- **Check which exams you need.** Mijn Inburgering shows this. It depends on when your integration started.
- **Book early.** It can take more than 6 weeks before there is a place for your exam.
- **Have DigiD ready.** See [how to log in to Mijn Inburgering](/guides/mijn-inburgering-login/).

## How to book

1. [Log in to Mijn Inburgering](/guides/mijn-inburgering-login/) with DigiD.
2. Choose to register for an exam.
3. Choose the exam: a language exam at A2 level (Reading, Listening, Writing or Speaking), KNM or ONA.
4. Choose a date and an exam location.
5. Pay. You can also choose to pay with your loan for integration, if you have one.
6. You get a confirmation by email and by letter.

## Changing or cancelling

You can change the date or location in Mijn Inburgering up to **1 week before** the exam. If you cancel up to 1 week before, you get your money back. Check the exact rules on DUO's [registration page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/registering.jsp) before you change anything.

## What it costs

Each exam has its own price. DUO lists the current prices on [How much does integration cost?](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/paying-for-integration/). Some people do not have to pay for their first attempts, for example asylum permit holders under certain conditions; the cost page explains when.

## Adapted exam for illness or disability

If you need an adapted exam because of an illness or disability, you do not register in Mijn Inburgering. You use the paper form *Aanmelding inburgeringsexamen* instead. DUO explains this on its [registration page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/registering.jsp).

## B1 or B2 instead of A2?

The state exam Dutch as a second language (Staatsexamen NT2, levels B1 and B2) is a different exam with its own registration through DUO. This guide is about the A2, KNM and ONA exams.
