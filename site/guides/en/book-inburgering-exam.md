You book the A2 language exams and the KNM exam yourself, in **Mijn Inburgering**. You choose the exam, a date and a location.

## Before you book

- **Check which exams you need.** Mijn Inburgering shows which exams you have to take and at which level.
- **Book early.** It can take more than 6 weeks before you can take the exam.
- **Have DigiD ready.** See [how to log in to Mijn Inburgering](/guides/mijn-inburgering-login/).

## How to book

1. [Log in to Mijn Inburgering](/guides/mijn-inburgering-login/) with DigiD.
2. Choose to register for an exam.
3. Choose the exam: a language exam at A2 level (Reading, Listening, Writing or Speaking) or KNM.
4. Choose a date and an exam location.
5. Pay. If you have a loan from DUO, you can choose to pay with your loan here.
6. You get a confirmation by email and by letter.

You can see the date of your exam in Mijn Inburgering. About **1 week before** the exam, DUO also sends you a letter with the date, time and place.

## Changing or cancelling

- In Mijn Inburgering you can change the date or location of your exam up to **1 week before** the exam.
- You can also cancel. If you cancel up to 1 week before the exam, you get your money back.
- No Mijn Inburgering? Send a letter to *DUO Servicecentrum Inburgering, Postbus 764, 9700 AT Groningen* and ask for a new date. DUO must receive it at least 7 days before the exam.

## What it costs

Each exam costs **€50**: Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking and KNM. If you are an asylum status holder, your first 2 attempts at each exam are free, but not if you take the exam at a lower level than your PIP (your integration plan with the municipality) says. The prices are on DUO's page [How much does integration cost?](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/paying-for-integration/)

## Booking with a paper form

If you cannot log in to Mijn Inburgering, use the paper form *Aanmelding inburgeringsexamen* from DUO's [registration page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/registering.jsp). Tick the box on the form if you want to pay with your loan. After you have paid, DUO sends you a letter with the date, time and place within 3 weeks.

## Adapted exam for illness or disability

If an illness or disability makes it hard to take the exam in the normal way, DUO can sometimes adapt it:

1. Ask for it in Mijn Inburgering with the form *Aanvraag advies aangepast examen*.
2. A doctor from Argonaut examines you and tells DUO whether you can get an adapted exam.
3. If you can, you book that exam with the paper form *Aanmelding inburgeringsexamen*, not in Mijn Inburgering.

DUO explains this on its page [Adjusted exam in case of illness or disability](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/ill-or-handicapped.jsp).

## B1 or B2 instead of A2?

The language exams at B1 and B2 are the state exam Dutch as a second language (Staatsexamen Nt2). You book those in Mijn DUO, not in Mijn Inburgering. This guide is about the A2 and KNM exams.
