**Mijn Inburgering** is DUO's personal website for people who are integrating. You use it to see which exams you have to take, to book exams, to read letters from DUO and to see your results.

## What you need

- **DigiD.** This is the Dutch government login. You need the **DigiD app** or DigiD with a **text-message check** (a code by SMS). A username and password alone are not enough.
- No DigiD yet? Request it at [digid.nl](https://www.digid.nl/en).

## How to log in

1. Go to the official [Mijn Inburgering login page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/log-in-to-mijn-inburgering/) on inburgeren.nl.
2. Choose to log in with DigiD.
3. On the DigiD screen, choose the DigiD app or a text message, and confirm.
4. You are now in Mijn Inburgering.

Always start from inburgeren.nl or digid.nl. Never log in through a link in an email or text message you did not expect, and never give your DigiD to someone else, also not to a school or a helper.

## What you find in Mijn Inburgering

- Which exams you have to take, and at which level.
- Booking an exam, and changing its date, time or location.
- Your exam results, and your diploma when you have passed everything.
- Your letters from DUO and your bills.
- Your personal details. Keep your address, email and phone number correct, so DUO can reach you.
- Your loan from DUO, if you have one: applying for it and seeing the amount.

## Mijn Inburgering or Mijn DUO?

They are two different websites. You book the A2 language exams and KNM in **Mijn Inburgering**. You book the state exam Dutch as a second language (Staatsexamen Nt2, levels B1 and B2) in **Mijn DUO**.

## When logging in does not work

- **Problems with DigiD itself** (forgotten password, new phone, blocked account): the [DigiD website](https://www.digid.nl/en) explains what to do.
- **You cannot log in to Mijn Inburgering at all**: you can still book an exam with the paper form *Aanmelding inburgeringsexamen*. DUO has it on its [registration page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/registering.jsp). See also [how to book your exam](/guides/book-inburgering-exam/).
- **Still stuck?** Contact DUO through [inburgeren.nl](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/).
