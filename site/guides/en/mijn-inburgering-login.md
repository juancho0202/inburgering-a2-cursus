**Mijn Inburgering** is DUO's personal website for your integration. You use it to see which exams you have to do, to book exams, to read letters from DUO and to see your results.

## What you need

- **DigiD.** This is the Dutch government login. You need the **DigiD app** or DigiD with **SMS verification** (a code by text message). A username and password alone are not enough.
- No DigiD yet? Apply at [digid.nl](https://www.digid.nl/en). DigiD sends an activation letter by post to your address in the Netherlands, so it can take a few days.

## How to log in

1. Go to the official [Mijn Inburgering login page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/log-in-to-mijn-inburgering/) on inburgeren.nl.
2. Choose to log in with DigiD.
3. Confirm with the DigiD app (scan the code with your phone), or with your password and the SMS code.
4. You are now in Mijn Inburgering.

Always start from inburgeren.nl or digid.nl. Never log in through a link in an email or text message you did not expect, and never give your DigiD to someone else, also not to a school or a helper.

## What you find in Mijn Inburgering

- Which exams you have to do (this depends on when your integration started).
- Booking, changing or cancelling an exam.
- Your exam dates and your results.
- Letters from DUO.
- Your loan for integration, if you have one.

## Mijn Inburgering or Mijn DUO?

They are two different websites. The A2 exams, KNM and ONA are in **Mijn Inburgering**. The state exam Dutch as a second language (Staatsexamen NT2, levels B1 and B2) is in **Mijn DUO**.

## When logging in does not work

- **Problems with DigiD itself** (forgotten password, new phone, blocked account): the [DigiD website](https://www.digid.nl/en) explains what to do.
- **You cannot get DigiD**, for example because you have no address in the Netherlands yet: some things can also be done with a paper form, such as registering for an exam. DUO explains this on the [registration page](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/taking-the-integration-exam/registering.jsp).
- **Still stuck?** Contact DUO through [inburgeren.nl](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/).
